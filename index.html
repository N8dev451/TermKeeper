TermKeeper

Start planning your future today!

TermKeeper is BrightMind Academy's academic tracking app, built to help you stay on top of your terms, courses, and assessments — all from your phone. Add your courses and up to two assessments each, jot down notes for study sessions, and get notifications before due dates sneak up on you.

Need a quick overview? The built-in report shows every assessment in a date range you choose, filtered by course so you always know what's due next. Your data stays on your device, so TermKeeper works even without Wi-Fi.
